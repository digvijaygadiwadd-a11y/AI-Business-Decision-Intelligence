# User Stories

# AI Business Decision Intelligence Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines business requirements from the user's perspective using Agile User Stories.

---

## Epic 1 : Executive Dashboard

### US-001

As a CEO,

I want to view overall company performance,

so that I can make strategic business decisions.

Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue displayed
- Profit displayed
- Active Customers displayed
- Churn displayed
- AI Summary displayed

Priority

High

---

### US-002

As a CEO,

I want to receive AI alerts when KPIs suddenly change,

so that I can immediately investigate business issues.

Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue Drop Alert
- Profit Drop Alert
- Customer Churn Alert
- Marketing Alert

Priority

High

---

## Epic 6 : AI Assistant

### US-010

As a Business User,

I want to ask business questions in natural language,

so that I can obtain insights without SQL.

Examples

"What caused revenue drop?"

"Which products have highest profit?"

"Predict next month's revenue."

"What is customer churn?"

Acceptance Criteria

- Natural Language Input
- AI Generated Answer
- Charts
- Supporting KPIs

Priority

High
